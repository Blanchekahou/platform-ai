# PlatformAI Personas

## Overview

PlatformAI is an AI-enhanced Internal Developer Platform (IDP) designed to serve multiple engineering teams across an organization.

Each persona has distinct responsibilities, operational challenges, and expectations from the platform. PlatformAI provides standardized capabilities that improve collaboration, reduce operational friction, and enable secure self-service across engineering workflows.

Rather than designing capabilities around technologies, PlatformAI is designed around the people who use the platform.

---

# Platform Engineer

## Mission

Design, operate, and continuously improve the engineering platform while enabling development teams through reliable, secure, scalable, and reusable platform capabilities.

## Responsibilities

* Build and evolve the Internal Developer Platform
* Operate Kubernetes clusters and supporting infrastructure
* Manage production releases and GitOps workflows where applicable
* Standardize deployment processes
* Improve platform reliability, scalability, and security
* Support engineering teams
* Automate operational workflows
* Continuously introduce new platform capabilities

## Current Pain Points

* Repetitive operational requests
* Manual infrastructure provisioning
* Frequent troubleshooting requests
* Context switching between projects
* Operational knowledge concentrated within a small group
* Limited time for platform improvements

## PlatformAI Capabilities

* Kubernetes Troubleshooting
* Product Provisioning
* Cluster Provisioning
* Infrastructure Automation
* AI Operational Assistant
* Platform Knowledge Search
* Workflow Orchestration
* Observability Insights

## Typical Workflows

* Provision infrastructure
* Coordinate production releases
* Investigate platform incidents
* Review deployment failures
* Improve automation
* Introduce new platform capabilities

## Success Metrics

* Reduced operational tickets
* Increased automation
* Faster incident resolution
* Improved platform reliability
* More engineering time dedicated to platform evolution

# Persona Relationships

PlatformAI creates a unified platform experience where each persona consumes platform capabilities appropriate to their responsibilities and permissions.

Rather than replacing engineering teams, PlatformAI augments their workflows, standardizes operational processes, and enables collaboration through secure self-service and intelligent automation.

---

# Guiding Question

When designing a new capability, ask:

> **Which persona benefits from this capability, and how does it improve their platform experience while reinforcing Platform as a Product principles?**
